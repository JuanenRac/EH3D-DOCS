# Architecture

The suite is a set of independent programs that share two libraries and one project file. The programs are written in Rust; the tools around them (the updater, the repository checks) are Python.

## Layers

1. **[EH3D-COMMON](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-COMMON)** defines what every program agrees on: the `.eh3dproj` container (a ZIP archive with `project.json`, `integrity.json`, `layers/`, `toolpaths/` and `vision/`), the telemetry types of a machine and the colours of the suite. It depends on nothing of the suite. The same repository holds the crates that the programs of the suite share: `eh3d-view` (the scene of a job, the themes and the words of the interface in eight languages), `eh3d-archive` (jobs in ZIP archives), `eh3d-bungard-formats` (the files of the Bungard machines and the Gerber and DXF outlines), `eh3d-controller` (the interface of the machine controllers) and `eh3d-filedialog` (the Open and Save windows).
2. **[EH3D-CORE-LIBS](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-CORE-LIBS)** reads the files that other people send (Gerber, Excellon, G-code). It depends on nothing but the standard library and has no window.
3. **The programs** (CAM, viewers, machine control, the launcher) are meant to depend on the two libraries and never on each other: they exchange work through `.eh3dproj` files. The programs that have started (EH3D-GERBERVIEWER, EH3D-ISOCAM, EH3D-STENCILCAM, EH3D-CAM-DEBUGGER, EH3D-LASER-CAM, EH3D-AUTOLEVEL, EH3D-PNP-MANAGER, EH3D-VISION-STUDIO, EH3D-TOOL-MANAGER and EH3D-SUITE-LAUNCHER) are a library and a command line each, tested on a computer, and use the readers of EH3D-CORE-LIBS or the height map of EH3D-COMMON; EH3D-ISOCAM also uses the shapes of EH3D-GERBERVIEWER. EH3D-SUITE-LAUNCHER finds every program by its manifest and starts it. Today EH3D-DRILLPRO, EH3D-ROUTEPRO and EH3D-BUNGARD-GUI read their files with `eh3d-bungard-formats`, ROUTEPRO reuses the protocol and the screens of DRILLPRO, and EH3D-BUNGARD-GUI and EH3D-MOTIONCNC draw their windows with the crates of EH3D-COMMON (themes, words, file windows).
4. **The machines.** **[EH3D-NODE-BUNGARD](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-NODE-BUNGARD)** is an ESP32-S3 node that sits in the box of a Bungard CCD machine and puts its serial port on the network; the firmware that every node shares is kept in EH3D-COMMON (`firmware_base/`). **[EH3D-SERVER](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-SERVER)** is the center: it takes the jobs of the programs and of the clients to come, plans them with the libraries of the programs and runs them on the machine through its node.
   **[EH3D-NODE-BUNGARD-AUX](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-NODE-BUNGARD-AUX)** sits on the drill head: it measures the height of the table with a laser, watches the speed and the temperature of the spindle and sends the camera like an IP camera (RTSP and ONVIF); the programs use its measures to build the height map of the table (`eh3d-levelling` of EH3D-COMMON) and correct the Z of the jobs; they reach it directly or through EH3D-SERVER, which relays its status, its camera and its measure with the token it holds.
5. **The phone apps** [EH3D-ANDROID-CONTROL](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-ANDROID-CONTROL) and [EH3D-IOS-CONTROL](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-IOS-CONTROL) are clients of EH3D-SERVER only: they read the fleet, the jobs and the node of the drill head through its API with the token of a person, and the emergency stop is the cancel of the running job; they do not talk to a machine or a node.
6. **[EH3D-UPDATER](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-UPDATER)** installs and updates the repositories on the PC of the person using them. **[ELECTRO-HOBBY-3D-UPDATER](https://github.com/JuanenRac/ELECTRO-HOBBY-3D-UPDATER)** sits above the updaters of the four ecosystems and dispatches to the one the person picks.

## Rules

* **A reader never panics and never guesses.** What cannot be understood becomes a diagnostic with the number of the line.
* **A file from outside is hostile until proven otherwise:** sizes, entry names and number formats are checked before they are used.
* **A program does not decide that a G-code is safe to run.** Limits, interlocks and the emergency stop belong to the machine and its controller.
* **Every repository describes itself** with an `eh3d.project.json` manifest; nothing keeps a list of projects, so a new repository appears by having a manifest.
* **Texts exist in eight languages** wherever a repository shows text to people.
* **Every behaviour has a test**, including the refusals.
