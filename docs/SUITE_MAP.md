# The map of the suite

What each repository of the EH3D Fabrication Suite is for, and whether it exists yet. "Published" means the repository is on GitHub with its tests and CI and a release; "Started" means the first code exists with tests that pass on a computer and there is no release yet; "Planned" means there is a design and no code that anybody can run. Nothing here has been run on a machine yet.

| Repository | Kind | State | What it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [EH3D-COMMON](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-COMMON) | Library | Published | What the programs share: the project container (`.eh3dproj`), the telemetry types and the colours, and the crates `eh3d-view` (the job on the table, eleven themes, the interface words in eight languages), `eh3d-archive` (jobs in ZIP archives), `eh3d-bungard-formats` (the files of the Bungard machines, with HP-GL arcs, Gerber and DXF outlines), `eh3d-controller` (the interface of the machine controllers, GRBL and a simulated one) and `eh3d-filedialog` (the Open and Save windows). It also has the height map of the table (`eh3d-levelling`) and the client of the node of the drill head (`eh3d-aux`). It also has the quality analyser (`eh3d-inspect`) that judges a picture of the camera of the drill head. |
| [EH3D-CORE-LIBS](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-CORE-LIBS) | Library | Published | Readers for Gerber, Excellon and G-code that never panic and report what they could not understand. |
| [EH3D-UPDATER](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-UPDATER) | Tool | Published | Finds, installs and updates the repositories of the suite on the PC. |
| [EH3D-DOCS](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-DOCS) | Documentation | Published | This hub. |
| [EH3D-SUITE-LAUNCHER](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-SUITE-LAUNCHER) | Desktop program | Started | Finds the programs of the suite by their manifests, starts them (again, a limited number of times, if they fail), keeps the recent projects and writes and checks the project file with the SHA-256 of every file. A library and a command line; the window and the discovery of machines on the network are not built. |
| [EH3D-TOOL-MANAGER](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-TOOL-MANAGER) | Desktop program | Started | The tool crib: drills, end mills, V-bits, engravers, lasers and nozzles with their wear counters and limits, JSON and CSV storage and starting feeds and speeds by material. A library and a command line; the catalogue of more than 450 bits with the 1/8 inch shank of the chuck lives in EH3D-BUNGARD-GUI. |
| [EH3D-ISOCAM](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-ISOCAM) | CAM program | Started | Isolation milling of the copper of a Gerber layer: the exact distance to the copper on a fine grid, several passes at the radius of the tool, a check for gaps that the tool cannot cut and G-code. A library and a command line; no window, no copper rubout and no board milled. |
| [EH3D-DRILLPRO](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-DRILLPRO) | Desktop program | Published | The classic screens of the Bungard CCD drilling software in Rust: Excellon files and jobs in ZIP archives, the manual and the basic automatic tool change, buttons that answer to the pointer, sounds at the end of a job and at a tool change, on a simulator or a real machine. Panels can carry tabs on their outside, a job can go on after a stop, and the plan can follow a height map of the table. |
| [EH3D-ROUTEPRO](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-ROUTEPRO) | Desktop program | Published | The classic screens of the Bungard CCD routing software in Rust: HP-GL files (with arcs and circles), DXF drawings and Gerber outlines, multi-pass paths, on a simulator or a real machine. The outside of a panel can be cut at its tabs, a job can go on after a stop, and the cut can follow a height map of the table. |
| [EH3D-BUNGARD-GUI](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-BUNGARD-GUI) | Desktop program | Published | The modern interface of the Bungard machines: the job in 2D and in 3D on the machine, more than a hundred materials of boards, eleven themes, eight languages, recent files, Gerber and DXF outlines, a window of the machines and stations of the server; it runs on the simulator of the classic programs. It also has a catalogue of more than 450 bits, the calibration of the table with a simulated probe, the window of the node of the drill head with its camera, spoken voices for the notices, tabs for panels, a scrolling list of tools with a colour for each, the board and the design moved apart, subtle animations and the job that goes on after a crash. It also turns the ring of LEDs of the head on and off and checks the work with the camera: the holes and the milled channels are judged and the verdict is shown as big as a sign. |
| [EH3D-SERVER](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-SERVER) | Service | Published | The center of the programs: it takes jobs from EH3D-DRILLPRO and EH3D-ROUTEPRO, plans them with their libraries, runs them on the machine through its node and tells every client how they go; tested against a simulator. It can check GitHub for newer releases of itself and of Studio and update them, through a helper started by systemd. It also lights the ring of the head, takes a picture, judges it with the profile of the machine and keeps the last quality check. |
| [EH3D-STUDIO](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-STUDIO) | Web application | Published | The configuration panel of EH3D-SERVER in the look of EH3D-BUNGARD-GUI: monitor (overview, machines by station, jobs, live events), configure (machines, users and tokens, the settings of the server) and account, in eight languages; the server serves its pages itself. Tested on a computer and looked at against a local server with simulated machines. An Updates page checks and applies the updates of the server and of Studio from the releases of GitHub. |
| [EH3D-NODE-BUNGARD](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-NODE-BUNGARD) | ESP32-S3 node | Published | Firmware that puts the RS-232 port of a Bungard CCD control unit on the network, with the machine's lamps, a spindle output and a web panel; the rules are tested on a computer and nothing has been flashed to a board. |
| [EH3D-NODE-BUNGARD-AUX](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-NODE-BUNGARD-AUX) | ESP32-S3 node | Published | The node of the drill head, with a wired network port: it measures the height of the table with a laser (the readings are filtered and a bad measure is refused with its reason), reads the speed and the temperature of the spindle, opens a protect output when the spindle is too fast or too hot, and sends the camera as RTSP (JPEG over RTP) and ONVIF like an IP camera, behind a login; the rules and the words of the network are tested on a computer, the firmware is not built and the drivers of the laser and of the temperature sensors are not written. It also drives a ring of eight RGB LEDs around the camera (white for the camera, colours for ok and not ok) and gives one picture on request. |
| [EH3D-NODE-BUNGARD-HMI](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-NODE-BUNGARD-HMI) | ESP32-P4 touch screen | Published | A 10.1-inch touch screen meant to control a Bungard CCD machine on its own, in eight languages with spoken alerts and orders; the rules (a gate with confirmation and a hardware-stop latch, the settings file, the voice) are tested on a computer and the firmware has not been built or run on a module. It has a numeric pad and a full keyboard of each language for the settings, buttons with symbols and notices by recorded voice. It also has a page for the quality check of the work and the ring of LEDs of the head. |
| [ELECTRO-HOBBY-3D-UPDATER](https://github.com/JuanenRac/ELECTRO-HOBBY-3D-UPDATER) | Tool | Published | A thin dispatcher above the updaters of the four ecosystems, EH3D-UPDATER among them. |
| [EH3D-LASER-CAM](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-LASER-CAM) | CAM program | Started | Laser engraving from a PNG or PGM image: threshold, Floyd-Steinberg and Atkinson dithering, raster G-code with dynamic power and overscan, and a power-against-feed test grid. A library and a command line; vector cutting and the window are not built. |
| [EH3D-GERBERVIEWER](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-GERBERVIEWER) | Viewer | Started | Gerber layers and drill files as an SVG picture with a colour and a transparency per layer, the narrowest track, the smallest gap between round pads and tracks, and a check that the layers line up and the holes are inside the copper. A library and a command line; the window with zoom is not built. |
| [EH3D-STENCILCAM](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-STENCILCAM) | CAM program | Started | Solder paste stencils from a paste layer: openings shrunk for the material, rounded, compensated for the laser kerf and checked against the aspect and area ratios, as G-code for a laser or HP-GL for a plotter. A library and a command line; nothing has been cut. |
| [EH3D-AUTOLEVEL](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-AUTOLEVEL) | Utility | Started | Corrects the Z of a G-code program with the height map of the table (`eh3d-levelling` of EH3D-COMMON), bilinear or bicubic, cutting the moves in pieces that follow the surface; arcs, G91 and G20 are refused. A library and a command line; the map comes from a simulated probe. |
| [EH3D-MOTIONCNC](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-MOTIONCNC) | Desktop program | Published | Machine control after the model of PlanetCNC, over a simulated controller for now: position, jog, zeros, a G-code program with its path, menus, settings, Open and Save windows. A program that was running can go on from the line it had reached after a crash. Real controllers (GRBL first) are not connected yet. |
| [EH3D-PNP-MANAGER](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-PNP-MANAGER) | Desktop program | Started | Pick and place assembly: the position files of KiCad and of CSV tables, the feeders and what runs out, and the order of the placements for a head with one to six nozzles with the bottom side mirrored, as a JSON job. A library and a command line; no camera and no machine control. |
| EH3D-NODE-HMI-GUI | ESP32-P4 touch screen | Planned | The same touch screen hardware as the one of the Bungard machine, as a general purpose manual controller for CNC, laser and pick-and-place machines. |
| [EH3D-ANDROID-CONTROL](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-ANDROID-CONTROL) | Phone app | Published | The workshop in a phone: the machines and their jobs in real time, the emergency stop (asked twice, operators only), the job in 2D and in an isometric view, the camera and the measure of the drill head through the server, sign-in and updates from GitHub, in eight languages. Tested on a computer; it has not run on a phone or against a real server. It also has the ring of LEDs and the quality check of the head, and its first screens follow the look of the other apps of the suite. |
| [EH3D-IOS-CONTROL](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-IOS-CONTROL) | Phone app | Published | The same app for the iPhone, ported from the Android one: the rules are a Swift package tested on a Mac in the CI; the screens have not been built or run. It also has the ring of LEDs and the quality check of the head, the same as the Android app. |
| EH3D-PENDANT-GUI | Touch interface | Planned | A remote manual controller for small touch screens. |
| [EH3D-CAM-DEBUGGER](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-CAM-DEBUGGER) | Desktop program | Started | The last look at a G-code program: its time with the acceleration of the machine and the alerts for rapid moves into the material, moves outside the table, cuts with the spindle off and heads over clamps. A library and a command line; the 3D simulation of the removal of material is not built. |
| [EH3D-VISION-STUDIO](https://github.com/JuanenRac/EH3D-VISION-STUDIO) | Desktop program | Started | Round fiducial marks in a PNG or PGM image, the scale from a mark of known size, the radial distortion of a lens and the turn, shift and size that put a board where its design says. A library and a command line; no live camera and no neural network. |

The layering and the rules every repository follows are in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md); what has actually been tested is in [CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md](CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md).
